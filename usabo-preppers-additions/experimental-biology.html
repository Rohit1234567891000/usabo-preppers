A DIFFERENT KIND OF BIOLOGY

Experimental Biology

USABO preparation is not only about remembering facts. You also need to interpret experiments, figures, graphs, and unfamiliar biological situations.

Experimental design

Identify variables, controls, replicates, hypotheses, and what an experiment can actually establish.

Graphs & figures

Read axes, compare conditions, recognize trends, estimate values, and distinguish correlation from causation.

Quantitative reasoning

Practice proportions, rates, probabilities, logarithms, units, and basic statistics when biology requires them.

Methods

Become familiar with common techniques such as PCR, electrophoresis, microscopy, sequencing, chromatography, and genetic manipulation.

A useful habit

When you see an unfamiliar experiment, first identify the biological system, independent variable, measured outcome, control, and pattern in the data.
