Study Roadmap

A practical sequence for moving from biology foundations toward competition-level preparation.

01 · Foundations

Scientific reasoning, chemistry basics, molecules, cells, energy, and experimental vocabulary.

02 · Cell & Molecular Biology

Cell structure, membranes, transport, metabolism, enzymes, signaling, gene expression, and molecular techniques.

03 · Genetics & Genomics

Mendelian genetics, linkage, recombination, population genetics, genomics, and inheritance patterns.

04 · Evolution

Natural selection, drift, speciation, phylogenetics, adaptation, and evolutionary reasoning.

05 · Plant Biology

Plant structure, physiology, photosynthesis, transport, reproduction, development, and signaling.

06 · Animal Biology & Physiology

Major organ systems, homeostasis, nervous and endocrine systems, immunity, circulation, respiration, and reproduction.

07 · Ecology & Behavior

Populations, communities, ecosystems, energy flow, biogeochemical cycles, behavior, and interactions.

08 · Diversity & Systematics

Taxonomy, major lineages, comparative biology, and evolutionary relationships.

09 · Experimental Biology

Graphs, figures, experimental design, controls, statistics, and interpreting unfamiliar data.

10 · USABO Practice

Past exams, timed sets, targeted review, and repeated analysis of mistakes.

One principle

Do not treat the roadmap as a checklist you must finish perfectly. Move forward, practice, find gaps, and circle back.
