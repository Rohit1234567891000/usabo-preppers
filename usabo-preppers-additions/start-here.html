Start Here

A practical path from “I want to try USABO” to actually preparing.

1. Understand the competition

Learn what the USABO asks you to do, including question styles, time pressure, and the difference between knowing a fact and applying it.

2. Build your foundation

Work through major areas of biology rather than memorizing isolated facts. Use the study roadmap to see how topics connect.

3. Use good resources

USABO Preppers is a map, not a replacement for a biology textbook. Use curated resources such as the Bioly Guide alongside your normal coursework and reading.

Explore resources →

4. Start practicing early

Do not wait until you feel “ready.” Practice is how you discover what you actually understand.

Go to practice →

5. Review your weaknesses

After every practice session, ask: What did I miss? Why did I miss it? Was the problem knowledge, interpretation, calculation, or experimental reasoning?
