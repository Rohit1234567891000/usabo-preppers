MAKE A PLAN

Study Plans

Choose a starting point based on the time and experience you have.

First month

Build foundations, choose a core textbook, begin the roadmap, and start doing small sets of questions.

One week before the Open

Prioritize timed practice, error review, high-yield weak areas, and comfort with the exam format.

The day before

Do not attempt to learn an entire discipline overnight. Review your error log, key references, and practical test-taking habits.

After an exam

Turn mistakes into a study list. Record what you missed and why, then revisit the underlying concept before attempting similar questions.
